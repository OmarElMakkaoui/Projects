# TIPE — Classes préparatoires

The TIPE (*Travail d'Initiative Personnelle Encadré*) is the supervised research
project assessed at the oral of the French *grandes écoles* entrance examinations,
written during the two years of *classes préparatoires* on a yearly national theme.

| Year | Report | Topic |
|---|---|---|
| 2022 | [Reaction–diffusion modelling of epidermal wound healing](TIPE_2022_Reaction-Diffusion_Epidermal_Wound_Healing.pdf) | Sherratt–Murray model of epidermal cell migration and mitotic regulation; reduction to the Fisher–Kolmogorov equation; travelling-wave solutions and phase-plane analysis (minimum wave speed *c* ≥ 2√(Dr)); perturbation methods; finite-difference / Runge–Kutta simulation |
| 2023 | [Commercial clustering with Schelling-type dynamics](TIPE_2023_Schelling_Model_Commercial_Distribution.pdf) | Schelling's segregation model adapted to store location; gravitational (Huff) attraction and utility functions; gradient descent for a store's optimal placement; Metropolis–Hastings and Monte Carlo simulation of the stochastic version |
